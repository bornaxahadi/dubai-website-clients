About the clinic

Beauty and health care, all in one place

FAQ

Questions patients ask first

Where is GS Poly Clinic?

In Jumeirah 1, Dubai. Tap "Directions" in the contact section to open it in Google Maps.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 10:00 to 16:00.

Does Botox or filler hurt?

Most patients feel only a quick pinch. Numbing cream is used for fillers, and you can go back to normal activities the same day.

How long do fillers and Botox last?

Botox usually lasts three to four months. Fillers last six to eighteen months depending on the area and product.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. You can split the cost with Tabby or Tamara.

Signature technique

VASER liposculpture

VASER uses ultrasound energy to loosen fat cells before they are removed, so nerves, vessels and connective tissue are better protected. That allows finer sculpting around the abdomen, waist and back, and usually a smoother recovery than traditional liposuction.

FAQ

Questions patients ask first

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp message or use the form below and the team will arrange a time at Gargash Hospital.

Am I a candidate for VASER liposuction?

It suits people close to their target weight who have fat that does not respond to diet or exercise. Dr. Juan Carlos confirms suitability at the consultation.

How long is recovery?

It depends on the procedure. Most liposuction patients return to desk work within a week; a tummy tuck usually needs two to three weeks. You will get a personal timeline.

Where is the surgery performed?

At Gargash Hospital on Umm Suqeim Street, opposite the Dubai Police Academy.

Do you treat international patients?

Yes. Many patients fly in for surgery. The team can help plan your stay and follow-up visits.

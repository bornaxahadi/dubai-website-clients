Signature technique

VASER liposculpture

VASER stands for Vibration Amplification of Sound Energy at Resonance. A thin probe sends ultrasound energy into the fat layer, which loosens fat cells while leaving nerves, blood vessels and connective tissue largely intact. The loosened fat is then removed through very small incisions.

Because the surgeon can work closer to the muscle, VASER suits detailed sculpting: abdominal definition, a slimmer waist, smoother back contours and a sharper jawline.

1. Tumescent fluid

A saline solution with local anaesthetic is placed into the area to numb it and reduce bleeding.

2. Ultrasound release

The VASER probe gently liquefies fat cells, protecting surrounding tissue.

3. Sculpt and contour

Fat is removed through tiny incisions and the area is shaped to your proportions.

Traditional liposuction
  • Fat removed by force alone
  • More bruising and swelling
  • Less control near the muscle
VASER liposculpture
  • Ultrasound loosens fat first
  • Usually less bruising, quicker recovery
  • Precise definition and smoother skin

FAQ

Questions patients ask first

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp message or use the form below and the team will arrange a time at Gargash Hospital.

Am I a candidate for VASER liposuction?

It suits people close to their target weight who have fat that does not respond to diet or exercise. Dr. Juan Carlos confirms suitability at the consultation.

How long is recovery?

It depends on the procedure. Most liposuction patients return to desk work within a week; a tummy tuck usually needs two to three weeks. You will get a personal timeline.

Where is the surgery performed?

At Gargash Hospital on Umm Suqeim Street, opposite the Dubai Police Academy.

Do you treat international patients?

Yes. Many patients fly in for surgery. The team can help plan your stay and follow-up visits.
